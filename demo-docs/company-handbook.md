# Northwind Demo Co: company handbook

Northwind Demo Co is a made-up company used to demonstrate SecureSearch AI. Nothing in this folder is real.

## Working hours
The office is open from 9:00 to 18:00 on working days. Core hours, when everybody should be reachable, are 11:00 to 16:00.

## Equipment
Every new employee receives a laptop, a headset and a monitor. Lost equipment is reported to the IT desk within one working day.

## Where to ask for help
Questions about pay go to the HR team. Questions about tools go to the IT desk. Questions about this handbook go to the office manager.
