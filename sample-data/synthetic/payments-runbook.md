# Payments service runbook

## Alerts

If the payments error rate is above 2 percent for 5 minutes, page the on-call engineer.

## Rollback

To roll back a release, open the deploy tool, choose the previous version and press Roll back.

After a rollback, post in the incident channel and open a follow-up ticket.

## Contacts

The payments team is reachable in the payments channel during working hours.
