# Leave policy 2026 (made up)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused days up to five may be carried over to the next year.

## Parental leave
New parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave can be taken in two blocks within the first year.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is paid for up to 10 days per year. A medical note is needed after three days in a row.
